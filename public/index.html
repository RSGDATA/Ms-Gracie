Meet Ms. Gracie

I'm a children's music performer based in Dallas, TX. I bring live, interactive musical experiences to birthday parties, community events, school assemblies, and more — creating unforgettable moments that get kids singing, dancing, and laughing.

Whether it's an energetic sing-along at a backyard birthday, a themed performance at a community festival, or a heartfelt session for a hospital or foster care outreach, I tailor every show to the audience and the occasion. I write and perform original children's music, and I've got a repertoire full of crowd favorites that kids (and parents) love.

I also bring experience from working with children with special needs, leading music and arts camps, and performing as part of a Disney tribute band — so no matter the event, I know how to read the room and make it magical.
