Meet Gracie

I'm a music outreach performer with a heart for children. Through live, interactive musical experiences, I bring joy, connection, and emotional support to kids in hospitals, schools, and foster care environments.

As a performer with KidLinks in Dallas, TX, I adapt every performance in real time — reading the room, meeting children where they are emotionally, and creating moments that matter. Whether it's a bedside song in a hospital ward or leading a sing-along at a school assembly, music is my tool for making every child feel seen and heard.

I also write and record original children's music, including an original EP of self-written compositions designed to spark participation, creativity, and emotional connection.
